## About This Blog

**O11y Alchemy** explores the intersection of agentic AI and observability—how AI agents can move beyond chatbots to actually operate infrastructure.

The name references the alchemical tradition of transmutation. Here, I'm transmuting fragmented observability tooling into unified, AI-accessible systems.

These aren't concepts—they're working systems with demonstrated results.
